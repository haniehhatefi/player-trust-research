# Methodology

This document summarises methods as reported in each preprint. Differences between manuscripts are retained rather than merged.

## Shared analytical orientation
Both manuscripts use Inductive Thematic Analysis (ITA). The longitudinal manuscript cites Braun and Clarke (2006, 2021) and describes iterative inductive coding. The cross-cultural manuscript describes a sequential design in which themes developed from the Persian-language corpus inform analysis of the anglophone corpus.

## Study 1: Longitudinal Persian-language analysis

### Design and period
Qualitative longitudinal study covering 2022–2026. Reviews were collected from the same calendar month and 30-day period in each study year to reduce seasonal and short-term event effects.

### Data sources
- More than 52,000 publicly accessible reviews from mobile app marketplaces.
- Supplementary anonymised customer-support interactions supplied under a research collaboration agreement.

### Sampling
The manuscript describes purposive sampling. Eligible reviews:
- had a one-star or two-star rating;
- were posted within the selected annual sampling window;
- contained at least 15 words of substantive content; and
- discussed gameplay experiences related to fairness, outcomes, trust, or perceived system behaviour.

Reviews focused exclusively on installation issues, feature requests, account problems, or unrelated service concerns were excluded. Approximately 500 review and support data items were retained for detailed qualitative coding across five annual cohorts.

### Analysis
The manuscript reports familiarisation, initial coding, theme development, theme review, theme definition/refinement, and production of the analysis. Each annual cohort was analysed independently before cross-cohort comparison. Coding was conducted in the source language; English translation followed theme development.

### Positionality
The researcher identifies as a native speaker of the primary corpus language with professional experience in mobile gaming research, player support analysis, and community insight work. Reflexive notes and analytical audit trails are reported.

## Study 2: Cross-cultural comparative analysis

### Persian-language corpus
The manuscript identifies two Iranian Android app distribution platforms as sources of publicly accessible reviews. The corpus contained more than 52,000 reviews posted between October 2022 and October 2026. The sampling strategy focused on one-star and two-star reviews posted within a consistent 30-day October window in each year. Reviews required at least 15 words of substantive gameplay commentary. Installation issues, support requests, feature requests, and off-topic content were excluded. Approximately 500 reviews were selected for detailed coding across five annual cohorts. An anonymised support transcript corpus was also included.

### Anglophone corpus
The source corpus comprised 4,116 one-star and two-star reviews from an anglophone competitive mobile backgammon app-store listing in an Australian market. A stratified random sample of 750 reviews was selected, 150 per year from 2022–2026. The manuscript reports a fixed random seed of 42 and a minimum of 15 words of gameplay-relevant content. The 2026 cohort includes reviews through 6 October 2026. The app and organisation are not identified in this repository.

### Analysis and AI-assisted preliminary coding
The Persian-language corpus was analysed first and seven operational themes were developed. For the anglophone corpus, the manuscript reports AI-assisted first-pass keyword classification to generate provisional theme flags. The classifications were reviewed and interpreted qualitatively; they were preliminary aids, not final coding decisions. The manuscript cautions that automated classification may identify explicit language more readily than latent meanings or contextual nuances, so frequencies should be interpreted cautiously.

Persian-language analysis was conducted in the original language. The manuscript reports bilingual back-translation to check semantic consistency of translated quotations.

### Positionality
The researcher identifies as a native Persian speaker with professional experience in the Persian-language mobile gaming ecosystem and as a non-native English speaker with cross-cultural gaming experience.

## Comparability note
The manuscripts' Persian-language sampling descriptions are similar but not identical. The longitudinal manuscript describes the same calendar month and 30-day period in each year; the cross-cultural manuscript specifies October. The anglophone corpus is stratified across the calendar year. Denominators also differ: Persian-language complaint rates are expressed as a proportion of one-star reviews, while anglophone theme frequencies are reported within the sampled negative reviews. The cross-cultural manuscript explicitly cautions that these measures are not directly comparable.

## Details not supplied
The preprints do not provide raw datasets, complete item-level coding files, full calculation workbooks, or all details required to independently recalculate every reported percentage. This repository does not invent those missing details.
