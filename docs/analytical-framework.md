# Analytical Framework

## Seven operational themes

| Code | Theme | Summary of player discourse |
|---|---|---|
| T01 | Dice Manipulation | Belief that random outcomes are not genuinely random or are manipulated. |
| T02 | Pay-to-Win Mechanics | Belief that expenditure influences competitive outcomes. |
| T03 | Bot Opponents | Suspicion that opponents presented as human are automated agents. |
| T04 | Connectivity Sabotage | Interpretation of technical disruptions as disadvantaging players. |
| T05 | Predetermined Outcomes | Belief that match outcomes are decided independently of player actions. |
| T06 | Statistical Anomaly Reporting | Use of unusual sequences or numerical observations as evidence of manipulation. |
| T07 | Support Dismissal / Perceived Support Dismissal | Perception that platform responses dismiss or fail to address concerns. |

These are categories of player discourse and belief. They do not establish that the alleged system behaviour occurred.

## Mapping in the longitudinal manuscript

| Meta-theme | Membership stated in the manuscript |
|---|---|
| Perceived Agency | T01, T05, T06 |
| Trust Erosion | T02, T03, T07 |
| Statistical Misinterpretation | T01, T05, T06 |

**Source note:** The manuscript assigns the same membership (T01, T05, T06) to Perceived Agency and Statistical Misinterpretation. This apparent overlap is reproduced as published and requires author clarification if the manuscript is revised.

## Mapping in the cross-cultural manuscript

| Meta-theme | Membership stated in the manuscript |
|---|---|
| Perceived Agency Erosion | T01, T04, T05 |
| Trust Erosion | T02, T03, T07 |
| Statistical Misinterpretation | T01, T05, T06 |

This mapping differs from the longitudinal manuscript for Perceived Agency: the cross-cultural manuscript includes T04 and does not include T06 in that meta-theme.

## How to interpret the discrepancy
These are two source-specific published mappings. This repository does not propose a merged mapping or decide which one should replace the other. A harmonised framework would require an author decision and review of the underlying coding and analysis records.

## Interpretive dimensions
The manuscripts use the meta-themes to discuss:
- **Agency:** whether players perceive skill, decisions, and effort as meaningfully related to outcomes.
- **Trust:** whether players perceive the platform and its communication as credible, transparent, and responsive.
- **Statistical interpretation:** how players interpret sequences, probabilities, and apparent patterns in uncertain environments.

These are interpretive lenses, not validated psychological scales or mutually exclusive categories.

## Player Journey of Distrust
The longitudinal manuscript presents a ten-stage interpretive model: (1) first notable losing streak; (2) asymmetric outcome observation; (3) first suspicion of manipulation; (4) active pattern-seeking; (5) perceived confirmation; (6) support contact; (7) perceived support dismissal; (8) public complaint or warning; (9) temporary disengagement; and (10) return to play.

The model is not a deterministic sequence followed by every player. The cross-cultural manuscript presents a related model with some wording differences; consult each study document before creating a study-specific figure.
