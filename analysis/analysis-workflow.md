# Analysis Workflow

This document summarises procedures described in the two preprints. It does not claim to reproduce the complete working process or provide access to original coding records.

## Study 1: Longitudinal analysis
The longitudinal manuscript reports the following:
1. Collect publicly accessible reviews from the same calendar month and 30-day period in each year from 2022 to 2026.
2. Identify one-star and two-star reviews with at least 15 words of substantive content relevant to fairness, outcomes, trust, or perceived system behaviour.
3. Exclude reviews focused only on installation issues, feature requests, account problems, or unrelated service concerns.
4. Retain approximately 500 review and support data items for detailed qualitative coding across five annual cohorts.
5. Conduct familiarisation and initial coding.
6. Develop, review, define, and refine themes inductively.
7. Analyse each annual cohort independently before comparing cohorts over time.
8. Code in the source language and translate into English after theme development.
9. Maintain reflexive notes and analytical audit trails, as reported in the manuscript.

The manuscript cites the six-phase approach associated with Braun and Clarke (2006, 2021).

## Study 2: Cross-cultural analysis
The cross-cultural manuscript reports:
1. Assemble the Persian-language and anglophone corpora.
2. Apply the study-specific eligibility and sampling procedures described in [Methodology](../docs/methodology.md).
3. Develop seven operational themes from the Persian-language corpus through inductive analysis.
4. Apply AI-assisted preliminary keyword classification to the anglophone sample to generate provisional theme flags.
5. Review the automated classifications and interpret them qualitatively; the manuscript treats automated outputs as heuristic indicators, not final coding decisions.
6. Compare themes, discourse patterns, complaint trajectories, and platform-response structures.
7. Translate Persian-language quotations after theme development and use bilingual back-translation to check semantic consistency, as reported in the manuscript.

## Limitations
The cross-cultural manuscript notes that AI-assisted detection may identify explicit linguistic patterns more readily than latent meanings or contextual nuances. Frequency estimates should be interpreted cautiously.

The manuscripts do not provide raw data, complete code, full coding workbooks, detailed keyword lists, model prompts, or calculation sheets in this repository. These missing details are not invented here.

## Reproducibility status
This is a transparent summary of the procedures reported in the manuscripts, not a fully reproducible computational pipeline. The source data and restricted support materials are not distributed. Intermediate annual Persian-language complaint percentages differ between the manuscripts; see the study documents and [Data Availability](../DATA-AVAILABILITY.md).
