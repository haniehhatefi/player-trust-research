# Theme Definitions

This document summarises the seven operational themes reported in the two preprints. It is a public-facing descriptive guide, **not the original working codebook** or a set of newly created coding rules.

| Code | Theme | Source-reported meaning | Interpretive boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| T01 | Dice Manipulation | Belief that random outcomes are not genuinely random or are manipulated. | A reported belief, not evidence of RNG manipulation. |
| T02 | Pay-to-Win Mechanics | Belief that spending money or purchasing in-game currency influences competitive outcomes. | A player interpretation, not a technical audit of monetisation. |
| T03 | Bot Opponents | Suspicion that opponents presented as human are automated agents. | An allegation; opponent identity is not independently verified. |
| T04 | Connectivity Sabotage | Interpretation of technical disruptions as disadvantaging players. | A perceived pattern; intentional interference is not established. |
| T05 | Predetermined Outcomes | Belief that outcomes are decided independently of player actions. | A belief about agency, not proof of precomputed outcomes. |
| T06 | Statistical Anomaly Reporting | Use of unusual sequences or numerical observations to support manipulation claims. | The analysis concerns player reasoning; it does not validate each statistical claim. |
| T07 | Support Dismissal / Perceived Support Dismissal | Perception that platform responses dismiss or fail to address concerns. | A perception of communication quality, not an objective rating of all support interactions. |

## Naming variation
The longitudinal manuscript calls T07 **Perceived Support Dismissal**; the cross-cultural manuscript calls it **Support Dismissal**. Both names are retained where source-specific detail matters.

## Use and limitations
The themes are categories of discourse developed in the manuscripts. The repository does not provide item-level coding decisions, exhaustive inclusion/exclusion rules, or a complete annotation manual because those materials are not included in the public manuscripts.

No verbatim player quotations are reproduced here because exact public text may remain searchable and linkable to an individual. Descriptions are paraphrased.
