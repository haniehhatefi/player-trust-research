# Data Availability Statement

## Summary

This repository does not distribute the raw datasets used in the two studies. It provides public-facing descriptions of the research design, thematic framework, source-specific findings, and links to the public preprints.

## Materials described in the manuscripts

- **Persian-language corpus:** More than 52,000 publicly accessible app-store reviews.
- **Longitudinal qualitative sample:** Approximately 500 review and customer-support data items retained for detailed coding across five annual cohorts (2022–2026).
- **Support material:** Anonymised customer-support interactions accessed under a research collaboration agreement. These materials are restricted and are not publicly available through this repository.
- **Anglophone corpus:** 4,116 one- and two-star reviews in the cross-cultural manuscript.
- **Anglophone analytic sample:** 750 reviews selected for detailed analysis through stratified sampling, with 150 reviews per year (2022–2026).
- **Cross-cultural Persian-language sample:** Approximately 500 reviews retained for detailed analysis, as described in the cross-cultural manuscript.

## Available in this repository

- Research questions and public descriptions of methods.
- The seven operational themes and source-specific meta-theme mappings.
- Aggregate findings reproduced with attribution to the relevant preprint.
- Conceptual diagrams labelled as descriptive or interpretive.
- Bibliographic references and DOI links.

## Not available

- Raw app-store review exports or compiled review datasets.
- Customer-support transcripts or private communications.
- Usernames, account identifiers, contact details, or personal data.
- Client documents, product assets, operational records, or proprietary materials.
- Original coding workbooks, complete intermediate code files, or calculation sheets.
- A machine-readable dataset sufficient to independently reproduce every percentage.

## Reuse and privacy

Public visibility of individual app-store reviews does not automatically make bulk redistribution ethically or legally appropriate. Exact text can remain searchable and may identify an author. Raw reviews and direct quotations are therefore not reproduced here.

Support material remains restricted and is not made available in this repository.

## Reproducibility limitation and reported discrepancy

This repository summarises procedures reported in the manuscripts but is not a complete reproducibility package. The underlying datasets, detailed coding records, and calculation sheets are not included.

The two manuscripts report different intermediate annual rates for Persian-language fairness-related complaints. The longitudinal manuscript reports 3%, 8%, 10%, 17%, and 18% for 2022–2026; the cross-cultural manuscript reports 3%, 5%, 8%, 12%, and 18%. The shared endpoints are 3% and 18%. The underlying calculation sheet is not available here, so no definitive corrected intermediate series is asserted.

## Access requests
This repository cannot grant access to client-owned materials, private support records, or restricted datasets. Any request would require review and authorisation by the relevant rights holder and, where applicable, the client.
